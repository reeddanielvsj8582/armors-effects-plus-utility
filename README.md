## What It Does

Armors Effects Plus changes armor from a collection of independent items into a set-based system. When all four pieces of a matching armor type are equipped, the mod recognizes the complete set and applies its associated gameplay effect. Each material therefore offers a different reason to choose it during combat or exploration.

The available bonuses cover several practical situations, including protection from particular damage sources, improved movement and attack capability, and utility effects such as nearby ore detection or interactions with mobs. These additions are designed to remain lightweight while making armor selection more meaningful.

## Armor Set Effects

- A complete matching armor set activates its own special effect
- Wearing a full leather set removes poison
- A full gold set prevents piglins from becoming aggressive
- Chainmail armor damages mobs in the surrounding area
- Copper armor prevents lightning damage and may call down lightning when hitting a target
- Iron armor raises damage output while slightly reducing movement speed
- Diamond armor improves speed and detects ores nearby
- Netherite armor protects against fire, lava, and knockback
- Every armor material has a separate role determined by its set effect

## Minecraft Compatibility

Armors Effects Plus is intended for Minecraft mod environments and requires a full matching armor set before its effects become active. Wearing only some pieces of a set does not produce the same result.

Keep these points in mind:

- The utility is designed for Minecraft mod setups
- Its logic is based on complete armor sets rather than individual pieces
- Another mod that alters armor processing, damage events, or movement rules may change the observed behavior
- Confirm operation with the Minecraft version and loader configuration you are using

## Common Questions

### What activates an armor effect?

Equip all pieces from the same armor set. The mod looks for a complete matching set instead of applying effects to single armor items.

### Will combining different armor materials work?

No. The listed abilities require a full set made from the same armor type.

### Are the effects customizable?

If your build provides configuration options or lets you edit the source, the effect logic can be adjusted for your setup. Otherwise, the included behavior remains unchanged.

### Why does the armor appear to have no effect?

First confirm that every piece belongs to the same set. Then check that the mod loaded successfully and that your Minecraft version and loader match the intended environment.

### Can it be used with other gameplay mods?

Potentially. Results depend on whether another mod also modifies armor behavior, combat events, movement, or damage caused by the environment.

### Where do I put the mod files?

Place the files in the add-on or mod directory used by your Minecraft installation, then launch the game as usual.
